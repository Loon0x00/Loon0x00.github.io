Loon4Mac DHCP Guide

October 20, 2026 · 7 min read
Loon0x00
Maintainer of Loon

Loon4Mac DHCP (Build 1007+) automatically assigns IP addresses, subnet masks, default gateways, and DNS settings when devices join the LAN. Loon4Mac can provide an independent DHCP service on a wired network and use global or per-device policies to decide whether each device uses the Loon gateway and whether Loon intercepts its UDP DNS traffic.

Loon4Mac LAN Gateway Guide

October 20, 2026 · 6 min read
Loon0x00
Maintainer of Loon

The gateway feature in Loon4Mac (Build 1007+) lets phones, tablets, computers, and other devices on the same LAN access the network through Loon on your Mac. Client devices do not need Loon installed. Point their default gateway to the address provided by Loon to use Loon's routing rules, policies, and proxy nodes.
